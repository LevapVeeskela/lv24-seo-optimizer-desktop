LV24 SEO Optimizer Desktop 0.8.0 adds Bing URL submission and read-only Bing status views on engine 0.10.0.

The Integrations screen can submit up to 10,000 URLs to Bing Webmaster, from a pasted list, the
project sitemap, or both. It is two-phase: review the plan, confirm, then apply. The engine sends
SubmitUrlbatch in 500-URL batches after checking the remaining daily and monthly quota. A success
means Bing accepted the URLs, not that they were indexed. A changed sitemap invalidates the plan, and
a missing site or key is reported as CONFIG_INVALID or AUTH_REQUIRED instead of a fake success.

New read-only cards show Bing URL submission quota (daily and monthly remaining, separate buckets),
sitemap status from GetFeeds (no per-URL errors) and crawl issues decoded from the provider bitmask
(no severity or date is invented). The desktop has no --site override: the target comes from
providers.bing.site. Read-only views do not auto-retry. Writes are not cancellable and have no
rollback.

The bundled engine is lv24-seo 0.10.0 with the Bing error-code mapping and 23 CLI commands; MCP
stays at 12 tools and the SQLite schema at version 4. MIN_ENGINE_VERSION is now 0.10.0, so an
installed 0.9.x engine is reported as incompatible.

Auto-update works from 0.2.0 for Windows NSIS, macOS .app and Linux AppImage; deb, rpm, MSI and the
portable ZIP are manual-only. 0.7.0 to 0.8.0 is the next in-app update. OS packages remain unsigned
(Windows/Linux) or ad-hoc signed (macOS); verify SHA256SUMS.txt.
